## what i dont understand

ive spent an overwhelming amount of time thinking about what reality is but ive realised it all 
comes down to one simple dilemma really. the way i see it there is two possibilities:

1# - reality has a start point
2# - reality is infinite

one of these has to be true but i cant make sense of either of them.

how can reality have a start point? if reality is 1 and non-reality is 0, how does 0 become 1?  
we know that our own reality (the universe we exist in) has a start point, commonly referred to as the big bang. but by reality i mean something greater than this. assuming the big bang is real, the question becomes, what came before it. we have vague answers to this too.
hot dense mass that expanded or whatever. but the question then becomes, how did THAT get there? if we say that, whatever existed before the universe expanded, actually existed, then that assumes an outside paramater that it existed within. the universe, logically, must have its own universe, in a sense. as i understand what 'real' means, to be real, you must exist within something. and if this isnt true, the universe exists of itself without anything outside of it, or something it exists within, then that is an idea i simply cannot
comprehend in the slightest.

you could say the whole multiverse idea solves this problem. and to an extent it does. it can explain how universes come to be, they are born of other universes. but then you have this issue of infinite recursion. which again, i cannot comprehend. as my mind understands it, there HAS to a top-level. a start point. something that gave birth to all things. but this is the problem. if there is, how did it get there? what gave birth to it? what gave birth to the thing that birthed it?

its the same question, regardless of what idea or theory you have about the nature of reality. how does 0 become 1. there is no getting around this dilemma.

the only way you can avoid it is... option 2. reality is infinite. there is no start. it existed, always existed, has no start or end point. i have little to say about this except.. i just cant comprehend the idea. i understand that 'time' as we know it started with the big bang. but this creates the idea that, before the big bang, there was existence without time. the word 'before' is null as the concept of before doesnt exist without time.

maybe i just dont understand the science on that one.. but it feels like such a cop out answer. its the same answer as many religious people have, god is infinite etc. but i dont get it. the way i see it, the idea of infinity is completely incomprehensible to humans. we dont know what it means, we cant imagine a reality in which something can be infinite. it's a concept we intellectualize but not one we
actually comprehend. so to this i say, how can you accuse reality of possessing properties you dont understand? if we cant explain what
it means for reality to be infinite, how can we even propose it as a theory?

but no. i do understand the science. the modern science says we have NO CONFIRMED DESCRIPTION of what, if anything, existed before the big bang. so it currently has absolutely zero opinion on these questions i have. science doesnt know.

I am VERY open to the idea of a previous universe collapsing into ours, some sort of eternally increasing universe, or some sort of cylical model where the universe expands and collapses on itself over and over. i think this fits perfectly with my philosphical model of how the world works. this is the Dao. the Dao describes everything as cylical. I like this. it would make sense the universe works like this.

but again. same question. how did the cycle start? where did it all come from? i think a scientist studied in this might say that my questions arent even valid because there is a good chance time didnt exist. if time doesnt exist, everything is infinite. again. im open to this. i just cant understand it. and i dont think anyone proposing the idea understands it. it feels like a cop out. if true, it means
reality operates in ways the human brain cannot ever comprehend, and should be entirely unknowable.

anyway. these are some brief ramblings into the things that go through my head every day. i think this serves as a neat summary of it all and why i cant stop thinking about it. every possible solution is incomprehensible... and there is something addicting about that thought. trying to get to the bottom of it is like having an itch that is impossible to scratch. i try to get closer every day. i never
make any progress.
